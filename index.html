Built for how India works.

Most AI assistants were designed for people who write prompts all day. Saku is for owners and operators who have a business to run and want the work back finished.

Languageभाषा
Brief it in Hindi, English or a mix of both. Reports and decks come back in the language you ask for.
Chatबातचीत
Talk to it like a colleague. Send a typed note, a voice note or a photo of what you wrote by hand, and Saku turns it into a plan.
Moneyपैसा
Priced in rupees. One monthly subscription covers your everyday work, and you can top up from ₹100 in a busy month.
Consentमंज़ूरी
Asks before it sends or publishes and keeps a record of what you approved. Designed with India's data-protection rules in mind.
Workकाम
Quotes, competitor checks, customer and investor decks, simple sites. The jobs small and mid-sized businesses keep putting off.

Access to your email

How payments work

UPI payments you approve

Every payment waits for your approval in your own UPI app. Saku sees that it was approved, never the PIN or the bank behind it.
