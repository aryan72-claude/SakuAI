Saku Agent

How Saku browses the web for its users

Saku is an AI assistant. When a person asks it to get something done online, it opens websites the way that person would, and does the task for them. This page explains what that traffic is, what it does on your site, and how to reach us.

What Saku is

Saku is an AI agent app, built for India, that works as one assistant for personal life and for work. People ask it to handle errands such as comparing products, shortlisting hotels and flats, and checking a business dashboard, through the Saku app and, soon, messaging channels.

Saku is a user-directed agent. Every browsing session starts because a specific person asked Saku to do a specific task. Saku does not browse on its own schedule, and it is not a search engine or a web crawler.

What the agent does in the browser

Saku uses a real browser to carry out the task the user requested. Typical actions are:

Search and compare

Searches shopping, travel and property sites, opens a limited number of result pages, and compares prices, ratings and options for the user.

Read and summarise

Reads pages the user asked about, such as product listings, reviews and quotes from suppliers, and reports back what it found.

Fill in forms

Completes forms such as enquiries and booking requests, using only the details the user gave it for that task.

Use the user's own accounts

Where a user chooses to share their own sign-in, Saku logs in as that user to fetch information such as a weekly sales report from their own dashboard.

Saku stops before payment. It can prepare a cart, but the user completes payment themselves in their own payment app. Consequential actions are confirmed with the user first.

What the agent does not do

  • It does not bulk-crawl websites, build a search index, or collect pages to assemble datasets.
  • It does not complete payments on a user's behalf.
  • It does not run on its own: no user request, no browsing.
  • It works through one user task at a time, and uses a small number of page loads per task.

If you want to restrict or block Saku

You are in control of your site. You can block or rate-limit the user-agent shown above in your server, firewall or CDN rules. If Saku is causing a problem, or you would like it to behave differently on your site, email us and we will look at it promptly.
